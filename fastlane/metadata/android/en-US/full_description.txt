Zero-Trust Calendar is a full calendar for your days, meetings and family plans. Month, week, day and schedule views, reminders that fire on time, and every event encrypted on your phone before it syncs.

WHAT IT DOES
• Month, week, 3-day, day and schedule views, with search.
• Repeating events with "this event, following or all" editing.
• Drag to move and resize events, long-press an empty slot to create one.
• Quick add in plain words: "Lunch with Dana Fri 1pm" (English and Hebrew).
• Several reminders per event, with snooze, directions and a "running late" message.
• A time zone per event, for trips and calls abroad.
• Shared and family calendars with other Zero-Trust users, end-to-end encrypted.
• Find a time and a public booking page: people pick a free slot, and the server only ever sees busy times.
• Public holidays and subscribed calendars, video-call links and attachments.
• Works with Zero-Trust Tasks, Notes and Contacts: due tasks, meeting notes, guests and birthdays.
• Open .ics invitations, import from Google Calendar (Takeout) and export any time.
• Optional: show chosen calendars in the phone's own calendar, or to desktop apps through a local bridge.
• "Up next" and "My day" home-screen widgets.

WHY ZERO-TRUST
• End-to-end encrypted: everything is encrypted on your phone before it syncs. We can't read it, and neither can anyone who breaks into the server.
• One private account for every Zero-Trust app, unlocked with your fingerprint or face.
• Works offline and syncs across your devices when you're back online.
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no data sold. Ever.
• Open source (AGPL-3.0), so anyone can check what the app does.
• English and Hebrew, with full right-to-left support.

Part of the Zero-Trust suite: Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Contacts, Phone, Fitness, Meds, Time and Browser. One account opens them all.
